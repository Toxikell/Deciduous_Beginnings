Text Analysis Capstone Project: An Exploration of Environmental Reproductive Health Language in Pronatalist Policy Document 'Mandate For Leadership Project 2025'

This project uses NLP analyses of 'Project 2025 Mandate for Leadership' to examine tensions between pronatalism and environmental deregulation. Results show mostly neutral language with ideological spikes around the FACE Act, minimal overlap between environmental and women’s health terms and weak environmental signals, revealing a policy disconnect between environmental regulation and healthy pregnancy outcomes.
